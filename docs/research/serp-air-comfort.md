## best-dehumidifiers
- Guides lead with pint capacity tied to room size and dampness level, and the 2019 rating change.
- Picks split by basement, bedroom, small room, cold room and pump models.
- Common questions: where the water goes, noise, power use, drain hose.
- Energy Star and continuous drain are compared.
- Gap we cover: compressor vs Peltier honesty and listing sq ft inflation.
## best-humidifiers
- Guides lead with cool vs warm mist, tank size and room coverage.
- Picks split by bedroom, large room, baby room, smart control and budget.
- Common questions: cleaning effort, white dust, night noise.
- Top-fill designs are flagged as easier to clean.
- Gap we cover: mineral dust and the ownership cost of cleaning plus distilled water.
## best-patio-heaters-under-200
- Top guides lead with fuel type (propane, electric, infrared), then BTU or wattage and heating radius.
- They split picks by space: open patios, covered porches, tabletop and wall-mounted heaters.
- Common questions: how many BTU do I need, can I use it indoors or under a cover, how long does a tank last.
- Budget picks recur around Amazon Basics 46,000 BTU and Hykolity or Sweetcrispy 48,000 BTU propane towers.
- Gap to cover: wind and open-air heat loss, the clearance and safety rules, and that electric infrared suits small covered spots only.
## best-budget-air-purifiers
- Top guides lead with CADR, room size rule of thumb and HEPA filtration.
- They split picks by small, medium and large rooms and extremely tight budgets.
- They note budget units lack air quality sensors and auto modes.
- Gap we cover: square footage claims versus CADR, filter replacement cost and H13 labels.

## best-budget-white-noise-machines
- HoMedics SoundSleep, LectroFan and Yogasleep Dohm lead lists; Hotmoon Cocoon cited as crowd-pleasing budget pick.
- Criteria: non-looping sounds, volume range, timer, plug-in vs battery; real-fan machines (Dohm) have a distinct sound.
- Caveat: constant sound at high volume is not advised near infants; keep machine at a distance.

## best-budget-smart-light-bulbs
- Wyze, Sengled, TP-Link Tapo/Kasa lead budget lists; Matter support and no-hub Wi-Fi are the common criteria.
- Wi-Fi bulbs 2.4 GHz only; many apps are required for setup; Hue is the premium comparison.
- Brightness (800 lumens) and color vs white-only are the main trade-offs.
