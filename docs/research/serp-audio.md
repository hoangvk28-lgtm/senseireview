- Picks split by flagship (Sony, Bose), budget, travel, Apple users and call quality.

- Gap we cover: ANC limits on voices and a realistic battery note.
## best-hdmi-switches
- Top guides lead with resolution and refresh support (4K at 60 Hz, 4K at 120 Hz, 8K) and number of inputs.
- They split picks by console gaming, home theater, bidirectional monitor sharing and pro matrix switchers.
- They answer auto-switching versus manual, CEC, HDCP and whether a switch adds lag.
- Several note that bundled remotes are the weakest part and a universal remote is often better.
- Gap we cover: HDMI 2.0 versus 2.1 bandwidth claims on cheap boxes, eARC passthrough limits and the powered-versus-bus-powered trap.
## best-bluetooth-transmitters-for-tv
- Top guides lead with low-latency codec support (aptX Low Latency, aptX Adaptive) and the TV connection (optical, 3.5mm, RCA).
- They split picks by dual-headphone sharing, optical input, travel and transmit-and-receive combo units.
- They answer lip-sync delay, whether a TV outputs PCM, range and pairing two headphones at once.
- Several warn that headphones must support the same low-latency codec or the delay returns.
- Gap we cover: Dolby or DTS output blocking the optical feed, codec matching on both ends and battery life while charging.
