## best-massage-guns
- Top guides lead with amplitude, stall force, noise, battery, attachments; picks split by overall, budget, mini, heat.
- Therabody and Hypervolt are frequent benchmarks; guides advise checking with a medical professional.
- Missed angle: speed-level counts are meaningless, light pressure use, battery wear; honest "who should skip".

## best-shiatsu-back-massagers
- Top guides split by format: chair cushion pads, pillow style kneaders, handheld shiatsu units; Comfier, Snailax, Zyllion, HoMedics recur.
- Lead criteria: number and movement of nodes, intensity levels, heat, auto shutoff, and which areas of the back each covers.
- Pillow units praised as compact; chair pads as covering more of the back.
- Many pages are dated and affiliate led, rankings disagree.
- Missed angle: chair height and backrest depth fit, node spacing versus torso size, cord length and power source, noise in office use, strap and cover wear, pressure being non adjustable on thin pads.

## best-compression-boots-for-recovery
- Top guides lead with Hyperice Normatec and Therabody JetBoots as benchmarks, then budget picks such as FIT KING and Renpho.
- Criteria led with: chambers or zones, pressure levels (mmHg or levels), wireless versus corded, app, sizing by inseam, attachments (hips, arms).
- Usage advice: use after exercise as an add on to cooldown; evidence on benefit described as suggestive.
- Picks split by overall, wireless, beginner, budget, most customizable.
- Missed angle: inseam and calf sizing mismatch, sequential versus pulsing patterns, noise of the pump, battery runtime on wireless, hose and valve wear, who should ask a clinician first (clots, circulation conditions).
