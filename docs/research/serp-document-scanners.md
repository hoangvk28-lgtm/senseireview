## best-budget-document-scanners
- Top guides lead with sheet-fed versus flatbed, then speed (ppm), duplex and OCR software.
- They split picks by use: receipts, home paperwork, portable, photos, and office batch scanning.
- Common questions: how fast, does it scan both sides, does it work on Mac and Windows, does software cost extra.
- Budget picks recur around compact Brother and Epson portables and a basic Canon flatbed.
- Gap to cover: that headline ppm is a best-case figure, and receipts and thin paper need a feeder built for them.
