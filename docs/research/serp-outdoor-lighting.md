# serp-outdoor-lighting

## best-outdoor-motion-sensor-lights
- Top guides split by power source (hardwired, solar, battery) and style (flood, spotlight, lantern or sconce).
- Criteria led with lumens, detection range (about 20 to 75 ft quoted), weather rating and adjustable settings; LEONLITE, Ring, Urpower, HMCITY recur.
- Smart home options (Ring) and decorative wall lanterns appear.
- Advice: mount height and aim matter as much as the settings; tune range and on-time.
- Missed angle: listed versus practical range, lumens per head versus total, wattage equivalent confusion, winter solar runtime, battery replacement availability, neighbor glare and aiming.
## best-solar-spotlights-for-landscaping
- Top guides lead with lumen output, beam spread and IP rating, then runtime.
- They split picks by garden accent, larger area kits, color-changing and budget multipacks.
- They advise comparing beam spread, IP rating and battery runtime.
- Gap we cover: LED count versus lumens, panel exposure and winter charge, separate panel designs and sealed batteries.
