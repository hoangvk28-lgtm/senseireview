## best-budget-pressure-washers
- Top guides lead with electric vs gas vs cordless, then PSI, GPM and cleaning units, then weight and portability.
- They split picks by job: car and patio furniture, decks and siding, driveways, and often name a light 1,600 PSI budget electric.
- Common questions: is more PSI always better, what does max vs rated mean, can it damage wood and paint.
- Several mention homeowner electric as the default recommendation and gas only for large or long jobs.
- Gap to cover: honest rated-versus-max PSI reading on cheap units, and why a cordless handheld is a rinse tool, not a driveway tool.
