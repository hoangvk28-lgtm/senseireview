## best-budget-smart-thermostats
- Top guides lead with HVAC compatibility and C-wire, then features: learning vs scheduling, sensors, voice assistants.
- They split picks by ecosystem: Alexa, Google Home, Apple HomeKit, and by home type with room sensors.
- Common questions: do I need a C-wire, how much will I save, is a subscription required, does it work with heat pumps.
- Budget picks tend to be Amazon, Nest and ecobee Essential models; many say savings come from a good schedule.
