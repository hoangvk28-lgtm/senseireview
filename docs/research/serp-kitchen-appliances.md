## best-electric-griddles-under-50

## best-popcorn-makers-under-50
- Top guides lead with type: hot air, stirring hot oil, stovetop and microwave, then capacity per batch.
- They split picks by use: healthy oil-free, movie-night volume, kids and nostalgia machines, and stovetop flavor.
- Common questions: oil versus air, unpopped kernels, how to clean, can I add butter and seasoning.
- Presto PopLite, West Bend Stir Crazy and Dash SmartStore recur as budget picks.
- Gap to cover: that air poppers blow light seasoning off and need melted butter added after, and that stirring-rod machines need careful cleaning.
