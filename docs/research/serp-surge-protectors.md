## best-budget-surge-protectors
- Top guides lead with joules, clamping voltage and UL 1449 listing, then outlet count and spacing, then cord length.
- They split picks by use: desk, home theater, travel, whole-house, and battery backup (UPS) for computers.
- Common questions: how many joules, how long they last, is a power strip the same as a surge protector.
- Belkin 12-outlet and Tripp Lite models recur as top picks; budget picks are 6-outlet strips.
- Gap to cover: clamping voltage matters more than joules, protection lights can mislead, and off-brand strips may lack UL 1449.
