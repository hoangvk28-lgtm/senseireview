# serp-firewood-storage

## best-firewood-racks
- Top guides lead with capacity (quarter, half and face cord), steel construction and weight rating, and whether a cover is included; Sunnydaze, Woodhaven, Amagabeli, VEVOR recur.
- Picks split by indoor versus outdoor, with kindling holders and carriers for indoor and covers for outdoor.
- Material notes: wrought iron durable, powder coated steel common; ease of assembly weighed.
- Many lists mix 2024 and 2026 dates and are affiliate supported.
- Missed angle: face cord versus full cord confusion, rack length versus log length, rack footprint on soil, covering only the top, rated load versus green hardwood weight, coating wear and rust at bolt points.
